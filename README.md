# AegisFlow AI

## Bitget-Powered US Stock & ETF AI Research Desk

AegisFlow AI is an AI-powered market research workspace designed for US stocks and ETFs.

The platform connects market intelligence with structured AI research:

**Question → Bitget Market Data → Context → Market Response → Signal → Risk → Actionable Insight**

## Core Features

- Search supported US stocks and ETFs
- Market price and historical market information
- Company fundamentals and financial information
- Market context and relevant intelligence
- AI-assisted signal generation
- Bull and bear case analysis
- Risk assessment
- Decision stress testing
- Natural-language research questions
- Structured research reports
- Human-in-the-loop decision making

## Bitget Integration

AegisFlow AI is designed around Bitget's official MCP ecosystem for supported US stock and ETF market intelligence.

The application does not invent market prices or claim unavailable data as real.

If a requested data point is unavailable from the connected source, the application should clearly indicate that the data is unavailable.

## Research Workflow

1. User asks a research question
2. AegisFlow identifies the requested stock or ETF
3. Market information is retrieved from the Bitget data layer
4. AI interprets the available information
5. Market response and signals are analyzed
6. Risks and alternative scenarios are evaluated
7. A concise actionable research insight is produced
